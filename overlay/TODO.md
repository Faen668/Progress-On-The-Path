## HDR

Built: ImGui draws in SDR onto a texture of ours, which `d3d12_hook.cpp` lays over the game's frame: as it is in SDR, or as scRGB or HDR10 (PQ), whichever the game gives `SetColorSpace1` (hooked as the swapchain is made). Blending is done as SDR does it, against paper white, so Dim the Game and the panel look the same in HDR. White is the game's `HdrPaperWhite` from `dx12user.settings` (Match the Game, on by default) or the HDR Brightness setting, capped at `MaxTVBrightness`. Turning HDR on or off while playing remakes our textures rather than stopping the overlay.

Tested off screen with a scratch harness, by turning each HDR picture back into SDR and comparing it with the SDR one: scRGB matches, HDR10 is within 3 levels (its 10-bit rounding), and a switch from SDR to HDR10 mid-session carries on drawing.

Left to do:

- Checked in game with HDR on (6 Nov 2026): looks right. Snipping Tool shots come out far too bright, as it saves the HDR picture as SDR without tone mapping; that's the tool, not the overlay.
- Change Paper White in the game's options with the window open: it's read again when the game writes `dx12user.settings`, which may only be when its options are left.

Strings are English only: every language's w3strings is a copy of `en.csv`, and translations are for the community to make and release.
